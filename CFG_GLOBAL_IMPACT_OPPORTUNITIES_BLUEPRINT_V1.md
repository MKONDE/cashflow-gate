# CFG Global Impact & Opportunities Blueprint V1

## Purpose
Public-facing programme framework for Cashflow Gate (CFG), covering community support, humanitarian assistance, agent and merchant opportunities, marketplace offers, entrepreneurship, partnerships and carefully reviewed investment information.

## Strategic allocation
- Strategic Growth & Social Impact Fund: **100,000,000 CFG**
- This allocation is within the existing **1,000,000,000 CFG authorised supply ceiling**.
- It is an accounting/programme allocation, not additional supply, cash, a USD valuation or reserve backing.
- The 100% verified reserve-backing requirement remains separate and unchanged.

## Initial 90-day growth period
Maximum planned deployment: **30,000,000 CFG**. This is a cap, not a mandatory spending target.

| Period | Maximum CFG | % of 100M fund | % of 30M cap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Month 1 | 12,000,000 | 12% | 40% |
| Month 2 | 10,500,000 | 10.5% | 35% |
| Month 3 | 7,500,000 | 7.5% | 25% |
| Total | 30,000,000 | 30% | 100% |

Unused amounts roll forward and are not forced to be spent. The remaining 70,000,000 CFG stays allocated for later approved phases and programmes.

## Growth Performance Gate
Before each monthly release, review verified active agents, merchants and customers; genuine commerce volume; fees and net programme cost; customer retention; acquisition cost; fraud/abuse; complaints; and social-impact outcomes.

## After day 90
Move toward a sustainable, performance-based incentive model. Reduce or discontinue launch offers that do not produce verified useful activity. Do not continue spending merely because a budget remains.

## Public disclosure rules
Clearly distinguish Planned, Approved, Active, Completed and Closed programmes. Publish approved budgets, reconciled amounts deployed, verified results and remaining programme allocation only after review. Protect beneficiary privacy and safeguarding information.

## Financial and safety controls
- No increase to the CFG 1B authorised supply ceiling.
- No use of this allocation as reserve backing.
- Preserve Treasury, Reserve & Issuance Engine V1 and its approvals/audit controls.
- Keep customer funds, reserves, company revenue and incentive allocations separately accounted for.
- Incentives require verified qualifying activity; prohibit fake accounts, self-referrals and artificial transaction loops.
- Investment returns are not guaranteed; actual outcomes can be zero or negative.
- Never pay investor ROI from new participant contributions.
- Any public investment information requires appropriate legal, risk and compliance review.
- This information page does not enable donations, payments, investment applications or payouts.
