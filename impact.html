ACCOUNTABILITY

How programme governance works

  • Each programme must have a defined purpose, eligibility rules, budget and approval record.
  • Rewards must follow verified qualifying activity; self-referrals, fake accounts and artificial transaction loops are excluded.
  • Monthly Growth Performance Gates review active agents, merchants, customers, verified commerce, fee revenue, retention, acquisition cost and abuse risks.
  • After the first 90 days, selected launch offers may be reduced and incentives move toward a sustainable, performance-based model.
  • Public reporting should distinguish planned, approved, active, completed and closed programmes.
  • Beneficiary privacy and safeguarding rules apply to all humanitarian and community programmes.
Important financial and reserve notice. The 100,000,000 CFG programme allocation is planned within the authorised 1,000,000,000 CFG supply ceiling. It is not additional supply, cash, a USD valuation or reserve backing. CFG's 100% verified reserve-backing requirement remains separate and unchanged.
